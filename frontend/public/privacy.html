Privacy Policy Touchline SA

Effective date: 9 September 2026

Touchline SA ("we", "us", "our") is a South African football application that provides live PSL scores, fixtures, results, league standings, player statistics, squad building, and football news. This privacy policy explains how we handle information when you use the Touchline SA mobile application ("the App").

1. Information We Collect

Touchline SA is designed to be used without creating an account. We do not require you to register, log in, or provide any personal information to use the App.

We do not collect:

  • Names, email addresses, or usernames
  • Passwords or authentication credentials
  • Phone numbers or contact information
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Payment or financial information
  • Photos, videos, or files from your device

2. Automatically Collected Technical Data

When the App communicates with our servers to load football data (scores, fixtures, standings, news), standard technical information may be recorded in server logs by our hosting provider, Netlify. This may include:

  • Your IP address
  • The date and time of your request
  • The type of request made
  • Your device's user-agent string (browser/OS version)

This data is collected automatically by the hosting infrastructure and is not used by us to identify individual users. It is retained according to Netlify's standard data retention policies.

3. Local Storage

The App may store a small amount of non-personal data on your device (such as whether you have dismissed the app install prompt). This data stays on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

4. Third-Party Services

Our backend servers use the following third-party services to provide football data. These services operate on our servers and do not interact directly with your device:

  • Supabase — Database hosting for football statistics, fixtures, and standings
  • Sportmonks — Football data provider for player statistics, match results, and league data
  • Netlify — Web hosting and serverless functions

The App does not embed any third-party analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, crash-reporting tools, or social media tracking pixels.

5. Children's Privacy

Touchline SA does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since the App does not collect any personal information from any user, no special provisions for children are required.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, rent, or otherwise share any user information with third parties. Since we do not collect personal information, there is no personal data to share.

7. Data Security

All communication between the App and our servers is encrypted using HTTPS (TLS). No personal data is stored on our servers because no personal data is collected.

8. Your Rights

Since Touchline SA does not collect personal information, there is generally no personal data for us to provide, correct, or delete. If you believe we hold any information about you and wish to enquire, you may contact us at the address below.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. If we make material changes, the updated policy will be published at this URL and the effective date will be revised. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
